Winmar Chain

Public RPC Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Scope: Winmar Chain mainnet public JSON-RPC endpoints

Information processed

When a wallet or application calls a public RPC endpoint, the service and its hosting or network providers may process the caller's IP address, request time, user-agent or client metadata, JSON-RPC method, request parameters, response status, and diagnostic or security events. Request parameters can contain public wallet addresses, transaction data, or other information supplied by the calling application.

Blockchain transactions and addresses written on Winmar Chain are public and persistent by design. Do not submit passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or other secrets to an RPC endpoint. Wallets should sign transactions locally and send only the signed transaction.

Purposes

Operational request data is used to provide RPC access, protect service availability, detect abuse, troubleshoot errors, and maintain network security. This policy does not represent that the RPC service can identify a wallet owner from a public address.

Logs and retention

Access, diagnostic, and security logs may be generated by the RPC service, hosting provider, or network-protection provider. They should be limited to operational and security needs and retained only as long as necessary for those purposes. A single fixed retention period has not yet been independently verified across all infrastructure providers; this policy therefore does not promise a specific number of days. Provider-specific handling may also apply.

Sharing

Information may be processed by infrastructure providers needed to deliver and protect the service, or disclosed when required by law or necessary to investigate abuse or protect the network. Winmar Chain does not sell RPC request data through this service.

Official endpoints

  • Primary RPC: rpc.winmarchain.io
  • Backup RPC: rpc.winmarchain.com

The backup hostname is an alternate access URL and is not represented as an independently operated disaster-recovery network. Endpoint availability and provider-side logging can change as infrastructure changes.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service or its data handling changes. For privacy questions, contact admin@winmarchain.io.

This document describes the public RPC service only; it is not a privacy policy for third-party wallets, explorers, bridges, or websites linked from Winmar Chain.
